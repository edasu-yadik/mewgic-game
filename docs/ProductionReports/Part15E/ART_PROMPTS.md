# PART 15E · Sanat üretimi

Yerleşik ImageGen aracıyla 11 bağımsız 1024×1536 atlas üretildi. Unity her atlası dört farklı teras olarak örnekler. Kristal Şelaleler’in beş mevcut çizimi kullanıldı. Yeni atlaslar `Assets/Resources/Map/Journeys` altında. Seviye taşları, numaralar ve kedi Unity’nin ayrı öğeleridir.

## Ortak prompt

> Production texture atlas for a fantasy mobile game's level-map scenery. Portrait 2:3 whole image. Layout is strictly a flush 2x2 grid with four perfectly equal edge-to-edge portrait rectangles (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right). No gutters, frames or margins. Each quadrant is a separate unique location in the same world, from an elevated 3/4 angle. Scenery is richly painted, dimensional, crisp, professional high-end mobile game concept art. Every quadrant MUST contain ONE continuous clearly visible broad pale trail winding down vertically from the midpoint of its top edge to the midpoint of its bottom edge, never leaving its quadrant. The travel lane is fairly central and winds gently: approximately center at top, x=0.58 near quarter height, x=0.44 near half-height, x=0.56 near three-quarter height, center at bottom. Keep the walking surface about 19% of panel width, continuous across bridges and clearly distinct from scenery. Avoid strong horizontal switchbacks. Make all four panels geographically different with different silhouettes and structures while sharing a coherent palette. Some include bridges over terrain or water. All scenery opaque, full bleed to every edge. No UI, level markers, text, letters, numbers, characters, symbols or floating buttons. Exact 2x2 grid at 50% width and 50% height. Theme:
