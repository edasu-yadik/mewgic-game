MeWGic Privacy Policy

DRAFT · Publisher, contact and support process must be confirmed before publication.

Version and scope

This draft describes MeWGic for Android, package com.mewgic.game, version 0.8.0, version code 11. It is based on the signed closed-test build. Publication date and publisher contact await confirmation.

Game information on your device

MeWGic stores level progress, stars, gold and diamonds, booster quantities, cosmetic ownership and selection, daily reward timestamps, tutorial progress, settings and generated puzzle records on your device. These records let the game restore your progress, apply rewards once and remember your choices. The game uses your selected language and local time for language selection and timed rewards.

Network, accounts and third-party SDKs

This closed-test build has no Android internet permission. Advertising and native purchases are disabled, and no production game-server address is configured. Google Mobile Ads and its consent library, Unity IAP 5.5.0 and Google Play Billing are included as dependencies. Their presence does not mean advertising or paid purchases are enabled in this build. Unity Analytics and cloud diagnostic reporting are disabled in the reviewed configuration. The reviewed game does not send player records, advertising identifiers or analytics to the publisher.

Accounts and online features

Basic play does not require an account. No working online account creation or game-server synchronization is available in this build. Clan, league and event interfaces that require a server cannot provide cross-device online participation in this build. Local event or reward records remain on your device.

Permissions and identifiers

This build does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising-ID permissions. It includes vibration and Android power/service permissions used by bundled runtime components. Optional vibration provides game feedback. Locally generated puzzle or transaction identifiers support consistent progress and rewards and are not transmitted to a game server in this build.

Storage and deletion

Local records are retained while app data remains on your device or in a backup controlled by Android and your device settings. To remove local records, use Android Settings, Apps, MeWGic, Storage, Clear storage. This deletes progress. Backup copies, if any, are managed through your device or backup-provider controls. Uninstall behavior depends on Android backup and restore settings. There is no publisher-hosted player account to delete in this build.

Support and store services

A verified public support and privacy contact must be added before publication. If you contact support, you choose which information to send. The support provider, handling and retention process require publisher confirmation. Google Play independently processes installation, store-account and diagnostic information under its own policies. This is separate from data transmitted by the game.

Changes

This policy must be reviewed before a release enables advertisements, purchases, analytics or online accounts. The policy on this page describes only the build identified above.
