## App Store subtitle

A magical sorting adventure

## Google Play short description

Sort colorful potions with a wizard cat and explore 12 fantasy islands.

## Full description

Choose a source bottle and a destination, then pour to group matching colors. Join a wizard cat as you solve puzzles and unlock winding paths across 12 themed islands.

Earn stars, improve your best results and collect coins and daily rewards. Spend coins earned in play on hints and other boosters. A paw guide demonstrates the first pour. Play offline and adjust music, sound effects and motion.
