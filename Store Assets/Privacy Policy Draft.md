MeWGic is a potion-sorting game. The current game stores your level progress, stars, virtual coins, boosters, reward timestamps, tutorial history, preferences and generated level layouts on your device. It uses the device language to choose a starting language and the local clock to make daily rewards available. You can change the language in Settings.

The game has no player account, advertising, analytics integration, paid in-app purchases or game-server sync in the reviewed configuration. It does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or notification access. Optional vibration uses your device’s vibration feature.

Your local game information stays on the device unless the operating system backs it up or restores it under your device settings. Local information is kept while the installation or a device backup retains it. To remove it, use the applicable app data or device backup controls. Removing the game or its data can erase progress.
